## Summary

DSP Trainer is an offline-first learning app. v1 defaults:

- No accounts or cloud sync
- No analytics or advertising SDKs
- No microphone access
- Progress is stored only on your device

## Data we collect

In the current v1 build we do **not** collect personal information, contact details, precise location, or usage analytics.

Local progress (which modules you completed and practice scores) stays on-device via the platform’s local storage. Clearing app data or using **Reset local progress** in Settings removes it.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share learner data with third parties in v1. Cloud builds distributed via Expo Application Services (EAS) / Apple TestFlight / Google Play internal testing may be subject to those platforms’ own terms when you install through them.

## Children’s privacy

The app is intended for audio / music-tech learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
